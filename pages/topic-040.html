Cost Optimization & FinOps: CUDs, Spot VMs & Billing Analytics

Maximizing cloud business value: FinOps lifecycle (Inform, Optimize, Operate), BigQuery billing export, Committed Use Discounts (CUDs), Spot VMs, storage tiering, and egress cost containment.

1 Real-World Explanation

The Situation: The Shock of the Unmanaged Cloud Bill

Moving to the cloud shifts costs from predictable capital investments to variable operating expenses. Without proactive governance, organizations experience "cloud bill shock"—where unattached persistent disks, oversized VMs, and accidental cross-region egress result in massive unexpected costs. [1] [2]

FinOps (Cloud Financial Operations) is the operational cultural framework that brings financial accountability to variable cloud spend. It follows a continuous three-phase lifecycle: [1] [2]

The FinOps Lifecycle
  • Inform: Establishing visibility. Exporting detailed billing data to BigQuery, enforcing mandatory resource labeling (environment, cost-center, application), and building Looker Studio dashboards. [1] [2]
  • Optimize: Taking action to reduce waste. Right-sizing oversized VMs, using Spot VMs for fault-tolerant batch workloads, deleting unattached disks, moving cold data to Archive storage, and purchasing Committed Use Discounts (CUDs). [1] [2]
  • Operate: Embedding continuous financial discipline into daily engineering workflows, tracking unit economics (cost per user or transaction), and setting automated budget alerts. [1] [2]

2 Technical Discussion & Architecture Diagrams

Layer 1 — Foundation: Granular Resource Labeling & BigQuery Export

Never rely on high-level monthly PDF invoices. Export detailed billing data directly to BigQuery. Enforce Organization Policies requiring labels on all resources to enable showback and chargeback. [1] [2]

Layer 2 — Practitioner: Spot VMs vs Committed Use Discounts (CUDs)

Use Spot VMs (60-91% discount) for stateless, interruptible batch processing. For predictable steady-state baselines, purchase Committed Use Discounts (CUDs): Spend-based (flexible across regions and machine types) or Resource-based (highest discount for fixed vCPUs/RAM in a specific region). [1] [2]

Layer 3 — Architect: Network Egress & Storage Tiering Optimization

Network egress is frequently the most overlooked cloud expense: keep traffic within the same region whenever possible (inter-region and internet egress incur charges). Use Cloud Storage Autoclass to automatically transition unaccessed objects to Nearline, Coldline, and Archive tiers. [1] [2]

Layer 4 — Staff: Unit Economics & FinOps Automation

Staff architects tie cloud spend directly to business value: "Our cost to process one checkout transaction dropped from $0.04 to $0.012." Automate non-production environment shutdowns (e.g., stop staging VMs every evening at 19:00). [1] [2]

4 Practice Exercise

Hands-On Demo: BigQuery Billing Query for Cost Anomalies

Run a SQL query against exported billing data to find the top 5 cost-generating services over the past 30 days.

SELECT
  service.description AS service_name,
  sku.description AS sku_name,
  ROUND(SUM(cost), 2) AS total_cost_usd,
  ROUND(SUM(usage.amount_in_pricing_units), 2) AS usage_quantity,
  usage.pricing_unit AS pricing_unit
FROM
  `my-billing-project.billing_export.gcp_billing_export_v1_012345_6789AB_CDEF01`
WHERE
  _PARTITIONDATE >= DATE_SUB(CURRENT_DATE(), INTERVAL 30 DAY)
GROUP BY
  service_name, sku_name, pricing_unit
ORDER BY
  total_cost_usd DESC
LIMIT 5;
