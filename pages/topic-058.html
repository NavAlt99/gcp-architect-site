2 Technical Discussion & Architecture Diagrams

Layer 1 — Foundation: Cloud HA VPN 99.99% SLA Topology

A single VPN tunnel provides no SLA. To achieve Google's 99.99% SLA, you must configure Cloud HA VPN with two tunnels across two separate gateway interfaces connected to redundant peer routers running dynamic BGP routing. [1] [2]

Layer 2 — Practitioner: Partner vs Dedicated Interconnect

Choose Dedicated Interconnect if your company already has hardware inside a Google colocation facility (meet-me room) and needs 10G/100G pipelines. Choose Partner Interconnect if you need less than 10 Gbps or do not have colocation presence. [1] [2]

Layer 3 — Architect: 99.99% Multi-Metro Interconnect Architecture

To qualify for the 99.99% Interconnect SLA, you must provision 4 separate circuits across two distinct metropolitan areas (e.g. 2 in Chicago and 2 in Ashburn) connected to two separate Cloud Routers. [1] [2]

Layer 4 — Staff: MACsec & Encrypted Interconnect

By default, Cloud Interconnect is private but unencrypted. Regulated enterprises mandate MACsec (Layer 2 encryption) on Dedicated Interconnect or run HA VPN over Interconnect to achieve line-rate encryption. [1] [2]

4 Practice Exercise

Revision Drill: Hybrid Connectivity Selection

Select the appropriate connectivity solution:
1. An enterprise needs to connect on-premises to GCP in 48 hours for a quick pilot with bandwidth under 1 Gbps.
2. A bank requires 40 Gbps private bandwidth with a 99.99% SLA between their colocation cage and GCP.
3. A retail company needs a 500 Mbps private dedicated link but has no presence in a colocation facility.

Answers

  1. Cloud HA VPN (fastest setup, runs over public internet with IPsec).
  2. Dedicated Interconnect (multi-metro 4-circuit topology for 99.99% SLA).
  3. Partner Interconnect (bandwidth < 10 Gbps via certified network provider).
